Taarifa za Uwasilishaji

Delivery Information

Tunawasilisha bidhaa Dar es Salaam, mikoa mingine ya Tanzania, na nchi jirani za Kenya, Uganda na Burundi. Muda wa uwasilishaji unategemea eneo lako:

We deliver within Dar es Salaam, other regions of Tanzania, and to neighbouring Kenya, Uganda and Burundi. Delivery time depends on your location:

Siku 1 · 1 dayDar es Salaam
Siku 1–3 · 1–3 daysMikoa mengine Tanzania · Other Tanzanian regions
Siku 3–7 · 3–7 daysKenya, Uganda, Burundi

Gharama ya Usafirishaji

Gharama ya uwasilishaji hutofautiana kutegemea eneo lako na ukubwa wa oda. Tuma ujumbe WhatsApp na eneo lako ili tukupe bei kamili kabla ya kuthibitisha oda.

Delivery cost varies by location and order size. Message us on WhatsApp with your location for an exact quote before confirming your order.

Njia ya Malipo Baada ya Kuagiza

Baada ya kuweka oda, tutakutumia maelekezo ya malipo (M-Pesa, Mixx by Yas, HaloPesa au NMB Bank) kupitia WhatsApp kabla ya kutuma bidhaa.

After placing your order, we'll send payment instructions (M-Pesa, Mixx by Yas, HaloPesa or NMB Bank) via WhatsApp before dispatching your order.

Ufuatiliaji wa Oda

Unaweza kufuatilia hali ya oda yako kwenye ukurasa wetu wa Kufuatilia Oda, au kwa kutuma ujumbe WhatsApp na namba yako ya oda.

You can track your order status on our Track Order page, or by messaging us on WhatsApp with your order number.

Sera ya Kurudisha Bidhaa

Kwa sababu za kiafya na usafi, bidhaa zilizofunguliwa haziwezi kurudishwa. Bidhaa yenye hitilafu ya kiwanda hubadilishwa bila gharama ndani ya siku 3. Maelezo kamili yapo kwenye ukurasa wa Vigezo na Masharti.

For health and hygiene reasons, opened products cannot be returned. Products with a factory defect are replaced free of charge within 3 days. Full details on our Terms & Conditions page.
