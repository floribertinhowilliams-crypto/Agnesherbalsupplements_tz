Privacy Policy

Ilisasishwa: Septemba 2026  ·  Last updated: September 2026

Agnes Herbal Supplements ("sisi", "yetu") inaheshimu faragha yako. Sera hii inaeleza taarifa gani tunazokusanya unapotembelea au kununua kwenye agnesherbalsupplements.com, na jinsi tunavyozitumia.

Agnes Herbal Supplements ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit or order from agnesherbalsupplements.com, and how we use it.

1. Taarifa Tunazokusanya

Tunakusanya taarifa unazotupa moja kwa moja unapoweka oda au kuwasiliana nasi — kama jina lako, namba ya simu, anwani ya uwasilishaji, na maelezo ya oda. Tunapopokea ujumbe kupitia WhatsApp au simu, tunahifadhi mawasiliano hayo ili kukamilisha oda yako na kukupa huduma bora.

We collect information you give us directly when placing an order or contacting us — such as your name, phone number, delivery address, and order details. Messages received via WhatsApp or phone are kept to complete your order and provide support.

2. Jinsi Tunavyotumia Taarifa

  • Kuchakata na kuwasilisha oda yako
  • Kuwasiliana nawe kuhusu oda, malipo au uwasilishaji
  • Kujibu maswali au maombi yako
  • Kuboresha bidhaa na huduma zetu

Hatuuzi wala kukodisha taarifa zako binafsi kwa kampuni nyingine kwa madhumuni ya matangazo.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties for advertising purposes.

3. Uhifadhi wa Taarifa

Taarifa za oda na bidhaa zinaweza kuhifadhiwa kwa njia salama kupitia mfumo wetu wa Firebase (Google Cloud). Baadhi ya mapendeleo ya matumizi ya tovuti (mfano lugha uliyochagua, bidhaa ulizoangalia hivi karibuni, kikapu chako) huhifadhiwa kwenye kivinjari chako mwenyewe na hazitumwi kwetu.

Order and product data may be stored securely through our Firebase (Google Cloud) system. Some site preferences (e.g. chosen language, recently viewed products, your cart) are stored locally in your own browser and are not sent to us.

4. Vidakuzi na Takwimu (Analytics)

Iwapo tutatumia zana za takwimu kama Google Analytics kuelewa jinsi wageni wanavyotumia tovuti, tunazitumia tu kuboresha uzoefu wako wa ununuzi — si kuuza taarifa zako.

If we use analytics tools such as Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, this is only to improve your shopping experience — not to sell your data.

5. Haki Zako

Unaweza kutuomba wakati wowote kuona, kurekebisha, au kufuta taarifa zako binafsi zilizohifadhiwa nasi kwa kutuma ujumbe WhatsApp au kupiga simu kwenye namba iliyo hapa chini.

You may ask us at any time to view, correct, or delete your personal information held with us by messaging us on WhatsApp or calling the number below.

6. Usalama

Tunachukua hatua za kimsingi kulinda taarifa zako, ingawa hakuna mfumo wa mtandaoni unaoweza kuhakikisha usalama wa asilimia 100.

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, though no online system can guarantee 100% security.

7. Mabadiliko ya Sera Hii

Tunaweza kusasisha sera hii mara kwa mara. Tarehe ya sasisho la mwisho inaonyeshwa juu ya ukurasa huu.

We may update this policy from time to time. The most recent update date is shown at the top of this page.
